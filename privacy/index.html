Learn to Fly with Aurora

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026 · App: Learn to Fly with Aurora (Android, package chat.dialex.aurora)

Learn to Fly with Aurora (“the game”) is published by Beatriz Guadalupe Gamarra under the developer name Dialex Lab (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: the game does not collect, share or sell any personal information.

What the game does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No real-money purchases. Coins are earned only by playing.
  • No chat, social features or links to other players.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • No internet connection is needed to play, and the game does not send data to us or to third parties.

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves the following on the device only: coins, unlocked pilots, upgrades, records, mission progress, chosen language and sound settings. This information never leaves the device through the game and is not sent to us. Android may include app data in device backups according to your own system settings; that is handled by Google under its policies.

Children

The game is designed to be suitable for children. It does not ask players for names, email addresses, photos, location or any other personal information, and it contains no ads. The “bonus gift” button in the game simply grants extra in-game coins; it does not show an advertisement and does not involve payment.

Deleting your data

You can remove all saved progress at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the game. Because we hold no data about you, there is no account to delete.

Third parties

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information about installs and the device under the Google Privacy Policy. The game itself contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send and use it solely to reply to your request. We do not share it and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Changes

If the way the game handles information changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before releasing that version.
